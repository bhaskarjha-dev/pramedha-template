# LinkedIn Exports

Drop your LinkedIn data export here.

**How to get it:** LinkedIn → Settings → Privacy → Get a copy of your data

Export only your own profile: pick Profile, Positions, Education, Skills, Certifications, Honors, Projects, Publications and Recommendations received. Leave out messages, connections and invitations: they hold other people's data.

Expected files:
- `Profile.csv` — Your complete profile data
- `Positions.csv` — Work history
- `Education.csv` — Degrees and schools
- `Skills.csv` — All listed skills
- `Certifications.csv` — Licenses and certifications
- `Recommendations_Received.csv` — Written recommendations
