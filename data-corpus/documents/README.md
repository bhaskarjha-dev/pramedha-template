# Career Documents & Formal Paperwork

Drop official verification paperwork, certifications, and institutional documents here.

## What Goes Here
- **Education & Credentials:** Degree certificates, official transcripts, diplomas
- **Certifications & Accreditations:** AWS, GCP, Azure, PMP, medical/legal licenses, course certificates (Coursera, Udemy, etc.)
- **Employment Proof:** Offer letters, appointment letters, promotion notices, relieving letters
- **Performance & Reviews:** Official annual appraisal documents, 360 feedback summaries
- **Awards & Recognition:** Formal certificates of achievement, hackathon prize citations, patents, publications

> ⚠️ **Privacy Warning:** This directory stores sensitive records. Never push these files to a public repository. Keep this repository private; these files are committed to it.
