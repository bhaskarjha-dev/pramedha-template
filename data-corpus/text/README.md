# Text Notes, Brain Dumps & Unstructured Context

Drop raw thoughts, career notes, transition brainstorming, and unformatted descriptions here.

## What Goes Here
- Career brain dumps: raw notes on what you've worked on, liked, disliked
- Unstructured project summaries: architecture descriptions, team dynamics, challenges overcome
- Email praise / Slack accolades: copy-pasted kudos from colleagues, executives, clients (with other people's names and contact details removed)
- Personal transition plans: notes about target companies, dream projects, ideal compensation
- Interview debriefs: questions you were asked, how you responded, areas to brush up on

## Why This Matters
AI assistants ingest files here to extract nuanced career narratives, STAR story context, and self-identified strengths that never make it onto formal bullet points.
