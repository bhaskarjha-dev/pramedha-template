# Resumes & CVs Archive

Drop every historical resume, CV, or biosheet version you've ever created here.

## What Goes Here
- Previous PDF resumes (chronological, functional, hybrid)
- Word documents (`.docx`, `.doc`) of past drafts
- LaTeX source files or Markdown resumes
- Academic curriculum vitae (CVs)
- Biosheets, one-pagers, or speaker resumes

## Why This Matters
Older resumes capture exact past dates, official job titles, team sizes, and early project descriptions that you might otherwise forget. AI parses these to reconstruct an uninflated, complete career timeline in `identity/experience.yaml`.
