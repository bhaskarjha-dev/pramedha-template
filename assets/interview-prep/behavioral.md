## 3. High-Frequency Situational Prompts & Frameworks

### Dealing with Failure
- **Opening:** Own the outcome cleanly without making excuses or blaming external factors.
- **Root Cause:** Explain the structural or process issue that allowed the failure to occur.
- **Remediation:** Share the immediate fix and the long-term preventive mechanism built.
- **Takeaway:** What permanent principle or mental model did you adopt?

### Navigating Technical Disagreement
- **De-escalation:** Shift discussion from opinions to testable hypotheses and metrics.
- **Evidence:** Use proof-of-concepts, benchmarks, or customer data.
- **Commitment:** Once a decision was finalized by leadership, commit 100% to making it succeed.

## 4. The 2-Minute Delivery Drill

Keep answers within 90 to 120 seconds to prevent interviewer fatigue:
- **0:00 – 0:20 (Situation & Context):** 1-2 sentences setting the company, team, and problem scale.
- **0:20 – 0:35 (Task & Role):** Your specific responsibility and the core obstacle.
- **0:35 – 1:20 (Action - The Meat):** The 2-3 decisive steps **YOU** took (decisions, trade-offs, execution).
- **1:20 – 1:45 (Measurable Result):** The business/technical metric achieved (%, $, ms, users).
- **1:45 – 2:00 (Reflection/Learning):** One concluding sentence tying back to the target role.
