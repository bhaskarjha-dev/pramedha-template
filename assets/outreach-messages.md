# 📨 Outreach Messages — Networking & Referral Playbook

> **Purpose:** Ready-to-customize copy templates for cold outreach, warm introductions,
> referral requests, post-interview follow-ups, and informational chats.
>
> **Derive tone and positioning from:** `identity/professional-summary.md` and `strategy/target-roles.md`

---

## 1. LinkedIn & Cold Outreach

### A. Cold Outreach to Hiring Manager / Engineering Lead
> **Subject / Opening:** Quick question regarding [Team/Initiative Name] at [Company]
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> I came across your work on [specific project/talk/post] at [Company] and was impressed by [specific technical detail or challenge solved].
>
> I am a [Your Target Title] specializing in [specialty 1] and [specialty 2], recently having [specific 1-line verified achievement with metric]. Given your team's focus on [team goal/domain], I would love to learn more about the engineering challenges you are prioritizing this quarter.
>
> Would you be open to a brief 10-minute chat next week?
>
> Best regards,  
> [Your Name]  
> [Portfolio/LinkedIn Link]

### B. Cold Message to Peer Engineer
> Hi [Name],
>
> I saw your recent work on [project/repo] and noticed you are on the [team/group] team at [Company]. I'm also deep into [relevant tech stack/problem space] and really admire how [Company] handles [specific architecture or scale problem].
>
> If you have a few minutes sometime, I would love to ask about what it's really like building on the engineering team day-to-day. No pressure at all, and appreciate your time!
>
> Best,  
> [Your Name]

## 2. Referral Requests

### A. Warm Referral Request (Former Colleague / Alumni)
> Hi [Name],
>
> Hope you're doing great! I saw that [Company] has an opening for **[Role Title — Job ID]** that aligns closely with my background in [domain/specialty].
>
> In my recent work at [Last Company/Project], I [specific achievement matching the role requirements].
>
> Would you feel comfortable submitting an internal referral on my behalf? I have attached my targeted resume and the direct job link below to make it as effortless as possible.
>
> Either way, great to stay in touch!
>
> Best,  
> [Your Name]  
> [Link to Role] · [Attached Resume PDF]

### B. Mutual Connection / 2nd-Degree Intro Ask
> Hi [Mutual Contact],
>
> Hope all is well with you! I noticed you're connected to [Target Contact] at [Company].
>
> I am currently exploring opportunities in [domain] and noticed [Target Contact] leads the [Team Name] group there. Would you be comfortable making a brief intro over LinkedIn or email? I’m happy to forward a quick 2-line blurb you can pass along directly.
>
> Thanks so much!  
> [Your Name]

## 3. Post-Application Follow-Ups

### A. Follow-up to Recruiter (Day 7 After Applying)
> Hi [Recruiter Name],
>
> I wanted to follow up on my application for the **[Role Title]** position (Application #[ID if applicable]) submitted on [Date].
>
> Having [specific top qualification or relevant project], I am especially excited about [Company]'s mission in [specific area].
>
> Please let me know if there are any additional materials or portfolio links I can provide to support the team's review.
>
> Thank you for your time,  
> [Your Name]  
> [Phone] · [LinkedIn Profile]

### B. Second Check-in (Day 14 / Approaching Ghosting)
> Hi [Recruiter Name],
>
> Checking in briefly to see if the team is still interviewing for the [Role Title] role. I remain very enthusiastic about the opportunity to contribute to [team focus].
>
> If the position has been filled or the search paused, no problem at all — I appreciate your time and consideration!
>
> Best,  
> [Your Name]

## 4. Post-Interview Thank-You Notes (Within 24 Hours)

### A. To Hiring Manager
> Hi [Name],
>
> Thank you for the conversation today regarding the [Role Title] role. I really enjoyed discussing [specific topic discussed during interview, e.g. how your team handles database migrations].
>
> Our discussion reaffirmed my excitement for [Company]. My experience with [relevant tool/framework] would enable me to quickly contribute to [specific priority the manager mentioned].
>
> Looking forward to the next steps!
>
> Best regards,  
> [Your Name]

### B. To Technical Interviewer
> Hi [Name],
>
> Thanks for taking the time to interview me today! I really enjoyed working through the [problem/system design challenge] and hearing your insights on [specific tech topic, e.g. latency tradeoffs in your pipeline].
>
> Best of luck with the ongoing project, and thanks again for a great technical discussion!
>
> Best,  
> [Your Name]
