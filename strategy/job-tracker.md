# 📊 Job Application Tracker

> **Purpose:** Track every job application from initial discovery through screening, interviews, offer, and decision.
>
> **Cadence:** Review and update at least once weekly during active job search.
> **Outreach Copy:** Generate follow-up and thank-you messages on demand from `identity/professional-summary.md`.

---

## 1. Pipeline Stages

| Stage | Emoji | Definition | Typical Timeline |
|-------|:-----:|-----------|:----------------:|
| **Identified** | 🔍 | Found opening, bookmarked, customizing resume | — |
| **Applied** | 📤 | Application formally submitted | Day 0 |
| **Recruiter Screen** | 📞 | Initial recruiter / HR screening call | Week 1–2 |
| **Technical Round** | 💻 | Coding, system design, or portfolio walkthrough | Week 2–4 |
| **Behavioral / HM** | 🤝 | Hiring manager, culture, leadership round | Week 3–5 |
| **Offer** | 📋 | Formal or verbal offer received | Week 4–6 |
| **Accepted** | ✅ | Offer negotiated and signed | — |
| **Rejected** | ❌ | Candidate declined or company passed | — |
| **Ghosted** | ⏸️ | No response after 2+ weeks / 2 follow-ups | — |
| **Withdrawn** | 🚫 | Withdrawn by candidate | — |

---

## 2. Follow-Up Rules & Automation Triggers

| Trigger | Action | Recommended Template |
|---------|--------|----------------------|
| **Day 7 after application** | Send polite inquiry to recruiter or connection | Generate from `identity/professional-summary.md` |
| **Within 24h post-interview** | Send personalized thank-you note | Generate from `identity/professional-summary.md` |
| **Day 14 (No response)** | Send second gentle status check; if silent, mark ⏸️ | Generate follow-up |
| **Post-rejection** | Send graceful acknowledgment; ask for feedback | "Thank you for the update. If possible, I'd value any quick feedback..." |

## 6. Career Contingency & Crisis Readiness Checklists

> Review quarterly. These are your emergency preparedness checks for layoffs, sudden market shifts, and career crises.

### If laid off tomorrow:

- [ ] **Financial runway:** ___ months of expenses covered (grounded in `strategy/financial.md`)
- [ ] **Resume ready:** Yes / No (last updated: YYYY-MM in `tools/resume/master.yaml`)
- [ ] **Network activated:** 3+ people I could call today (grounded in `strategy/networking.md`)
- [ ] **Emergency references:** Listed in `identity/references.yaml`
- [ ] **Portfolio up to date:** `identity/` and resume data current
- [ ] **Job search channels:** Know where to look (pipeline table set up above)

### If current role becomes unbearable:

- [ ] Documented specific issues (for reference in exit interview)
- [ ] Internal transfer options explored
- [ ] External market salary validated (grounded in `strategy/financial.md`)
- [ ] Notice period and financial runway confirmed
- [ ] At least 2 target companies researched

### If industry shifts dramatically:

- [ ] Adjacent industries identified (grounded in `strategy/target-roles.md`)
- [ ] Transferable skills documented (grounded in `identity/skills.yaml`)
- [ ] Reskilling timeline estimated (grounded in `strategy/study-plan.md`)
- [ ] Financial buffer for transition period confirmed
