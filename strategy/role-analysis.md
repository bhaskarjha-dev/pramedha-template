## Part 2: The Complete Role Universe

### Scoring Parameters (Each 1-5)

| Parameter | What It Measures |
|-----------|-----------------|
| **P1: Skills Match** | How much of the must-have skills do you ALREADY have? |
| **P2: Interest/Passion** | How excited are you about doing this work daily? |
| **P3: Market Demand** | How many openings exist? Is demand growing? |
| **P4: Salary Potential** | Does it meet your financial targets at your experience level? |
| **P5: Growth Trajectory** | Where does this role lead in 3-5 years? |
| **P6: Time to Close Gaps** | How long to become competitive? (5 = ready now, 1 = 12+ months) |
| **P7: Interview Readiness** | Can you pass the interview TODAY? (5 = yes, 1 = no chance) |
| **P8: Competition Level** | How crowded is the applicant pool? (5 = low competition, 1 = brutal) |

> **Scoring guide:** 5 = Excellent | 4 = Good | 3 = Adequate | 2 = Weak | 1 = Absent/Critical gap

### Tier Definitions (8-Parameter Scale, Max 40)

- **Tier A (32-40):** Primary targets — apply aggressively
- **Tier B (24-31):** Secondary — apply selectively or invest in gaps
- **Tier C (16-23):** Stretch — only if exceptional opportunity
