## 4. Ground Rules for High-Conversion Job Search

1. **Quality Over Quantity:** Limit cold applications to 3–5 per day; personalize each with a targeted resume variant.
2. **Never Apply with ❌ UNVERIFIED Claims:** Every bullet must trace back to `identity/claims-and-evidence.md`.
3. **Track Every Submission:** Immediately record submissions in [strategy/job-tracker.md](job-tracker.md).
4. **Follow Up Systematically:** Use the 7-day follow-up script in [assets/outreach-messages.md](../assets/outreach-messages.md).
5. **Leverage Warm Paths First:** A warm referral via an ex-colleague converts 10x higher than a cold ATS submission.
