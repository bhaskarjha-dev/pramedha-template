## 2. Business, Product & Strategy Track

### Case Interview Frameworks

| Case Type | Core Diagnostic Structure | Key Metrics |
|-----------|---------------------------|-------------|
| **Profitability** | Revenue (Price × Volume) vs Costs (Fixed + Variable) | Margins, CAC, LTV, churn |
| **Market Entry** | Market size, competitive dynamics, capabilities, financial payback | TAM/SAM/SOM, barrier to entry |
| **Product Sense** | User persona, underserved pain point, 3 feature bets, prioritization | DAU/WAU, activation, retention |
| **Growth Strategy** | Acquisition channels, conversion funnel, virality, monetization | Payback period, ROAS |

### Product & Strategy Walkthrough Template
- **Business Goal & Context:** What high-stakes business problem was solved?
- **User Discovery:** What quantitative data or customer research validated the need?
- **Trade-Off Decision:** What attractive idea did you actively decide *not* to build?
- **Impact & Measurement:** What metric moved, and what was the attribution?

## 3. Creative & Design Track

### Portfolio Presentation Structure (45–60 min)
1. **Context & Problem Statement (5 min):** Business objective and user challenge.
2. **Research & Insights (10 min):** User journeys, edge cases discovered, constraints.
3. **Exploration & Divergence (10 min):** Alternative directions explored and rejected.
4. **Final System & Craft (15 min):** UI details, interaction states, design system tokens.
5. **Business Impact & Reflection (5 min):** Measured outcome, what you'd do differently.

### Design Critique & Defense Checklist
- [ ] Rationale for typography hierarchy and color contrast accessibility (WCAG AA/AAA)
- [ ] Explanation of mobile vs desktop responsive breakpoints
- [ ] Hand-off documentation and developer collaboration model

## 4. Medical, Clinical & Life Sciences Track

### Clinical Vignette Presentation Framework
- **Case Presentation:** Patient demographics, chief complaint, pertinent positives/negatives.
- **Differential Diagnosis:** Prioritized by likelihood vs acuity (rule out life threats).
- **Diagnostic Workup:** Evidence-based lab, imaging, and biomarker justification.
- **Management & Disposition:** Multidisciplinary treatment plan, contingencies, ethics.

## 5. Academic, Research & Scientific Track

### Job Talk / Chalk Talk Defense (50 min)
- **Past Major Breakthrough (15 min):** Core methodology, publications, and impact.
- **Current Active Research (15 min):** Active projects, preliminary data, and tooling.
- **Future 5-Year Research Agenda (15 min):** Specific grant proposals, lab staffing, funding sources.
- **Teaching & Departmental Fit (5 min):** Courses prepared to teach, institutional synergy.

## 6. Legal & Policy Track

### Legal Synthesis & Issue-Spotting Checklist
- **Procedural Posture:** Jurisdiction, standard of review, timeline constraints.
- **Issue Spotting:** Primary statutory elements, conflicting precedents, regulatory posture.
- **Analysis:** Argument synthesis, counter-argument mitigation, risk exposure.
- **Client Recommendation:** Actionable business advice with risk-adjusted probability.

## 7. Take-Home / Deliverable Evaluation Checklist

Before submitting any technical assignment, brief, or case study:
- [ ] Executive summary or README with clear setup / execution instructions
- [ ] Tests written, documented, and passing cleanly
- [ ] Critical edge cases handled and explicitly documented
- [ ] Trade-offs, assumptions, and future enhancements noted
- [ ] Delivery format complies exactly with recruiter instructions
